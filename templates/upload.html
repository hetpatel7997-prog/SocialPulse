How to download your Instagram data

  1. Open Instagram (app or website) and go to Accounts Center from your profile menu or settings.
  2. Choose Your information and permissions, then Download your information.
  3. Select Download or transfer information, pick your Instagram account, and choose either All available information or Some of your information (tick Followers and following and Posts).
  4. Select Download to device, set the format to JSON and the date range to All time, then create the files.
  5. Instagram notifies you when the file is ready (this can take from minutes to a few days). Download the ZIP and unzip it.
  6. Upload the files you need, for example followers_1.json, following.json and posts_1.json (they sit under folders like connections and your_instagram_activity). HTML exports of followers and following also work.

Menu names can change slightly between app versions.

Instagram's standard export lists your posts but usually does not include like and comment counts per post. SocialPulse never invents them: those metrics stay unavailable unless your file has them. A CSV or Excel sheet with columns such as date, caption, likes, comments, type (for example from an insights export or your own spreadsheet) unlocks the engagement analytics.
